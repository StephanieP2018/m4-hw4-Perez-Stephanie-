About Me

Hello! My name is Stephanie Perez, and I am a passionate graphic designer with a love for creating beautiful and functional designs. I have experience in Adobe Creative Suite, including Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign. My goal is to build user-friendly and visually appealing web designs and applications that provide an excellent user experience.

I have worked on several design projects, both personal and professional. My experience includes creating brand identities, developing marketing materials, and designing user interfaces for various platforms.

If you would like to get in touch with me, please feel free to reach out via email or through my social media channels. I am always open to new opportunities and collaborations.
